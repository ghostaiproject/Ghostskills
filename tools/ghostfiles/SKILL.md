# Ghostfiles (files + visuals; chat replies → ghoststyle)

## Look
- Flat, clean, presentation-style: big titles, short lines, lots of space, one accent color.
- NO hacker, terminal, ASCII, monospace or neon look. No box-drawing characters.
- One idea per slide/section; numbers as large stat tiles, not paragraphs.

## Images
- Fit a phone screen in one view: default 1080x2340 portrait (or the target's exact ratio).
- Content fills the frame: no black bars, no letterbox, not taller than the screen.
- Wrong ratio → re-render, never pad.

## Examples
Neutral names only: Chalet, Condo, Bureau, Studio, Atelier. Never real people or family.

## Before sending
Render once, check: final size, readable on a phone, nothing cut, no ASCII leftovers.
Sizes, fonts, palette → SPECS.md.
